# ApexShield Care Plus — Policy Document (Fictional)

> **Notice**: This document represents an entirely fictional insurance policy created solely for the ClaimAssist AI demonstration and evaluation system. It does not represent actual insurance coverage.

---

## 1. Policy Overview and Eligibility
- **Policy Name**: ApexShield Comprehensive Health & Wellness Plan (Care Plus).
- **Policy Term**: 12 calendar months from the effective date stated in the Policy Schedule.
- **Eligible Persons**: Primary policyholders aged 18 to 65 years residing in the coverage territory, and their lawful dependents under age 26.
- **Coverage Territory**: Nationwide within participating provider networks and non-emergency out-of-network facilities subject to co-insurance terms.

---

## 2. Coverage Conditions and Covered Benefits
The policy provides coverage for medically necessary treatments prescribed by licensed healthcare providers:
- **Section 2.1 Inpatient Hospitalization**: Covered up to 100% of reasonable and customary charges after deductible. Includes room boarding (standard semi-private room), intensive care units (ICU), surgery, nursing fees, and inpatient prescription medications.
- **Section 2.2 Outpatient Specialist Consultations**: Covered with a fixed copay of \$30 per consultation for in-network specialists; out-of-network consultations are reimbursed at 70% after the annual deductible.
- **Section 2.3 Diagnostic Tests & Imaging**: Laboratory tests, X-rays, MRI, and CT scans are covered when ordered by a recognized physician. Pre-authorization is required for advanced imaging (MRI, PET, CT scans) exceeding \$500.
- **Section 2.4 Emergency Services**: Immediate emergency room (ER) treatment for life-threatening conditions is covered worldwide with a \$100 copay (waived if admitted as an inpatient).
- **Section 2.5 Prescription Drugs**: Tier 1 generics covered at 90%; Tier 2 brand-name formulary covered at 75%; Tier 3 non-formulary covered at 50% with prior authorization.
- **Section 2.6 Preventive Care**: Annual physical checkups, routine immunizations, and preventive screening mammograms/colonoscopies are covered at 100% with no copayment or deductible.

---

## 3. Waiting Periods
- **Section 3.1 General Waiting Period**: A standard waiting period of 30 days applies from the policy effective date before non-emergency medical claims are eligible for reimbursement.
- **Section 3.2 Pre-Existing Conditions**: Pre-existing conditions (any symptom, diagnosis, or treatment occurring within 24 months prior to enrollment) have a mandatory waiting period of 12 consecutive months of continuous coverage.
- **Section 3.3 Maternity & Childbirth**: Coverage for normal delivery, cesarean section, and related maternity expenses begins only after a 10-month continuous coverage waiting period.
- **Section 3.4 Specific Surgeries**: Elective joint replacement, hernia repair, cataract surgery, and gallbladder removal require a 90-day waiting period from the policy effective date.
- **Section 3.5 Accidental Injuries**: No waiting period applies to medical treatment arising directly from sudden external accidental bodily injuries occurring after policy activation.

---

## 4. Exclusions and Non-Covered Expenses
The following treatments and expenses are expressly excluded from coverage:
- **Section 4.1 Cosmetic & Elective Surgery**: Aesthetic procedures, rhinoplasty for cosmetic purposes, hair transplants, and elective body contouring unless necessitated by reconstructive surgery following an accidental injury.
- **Section 4.2 Experimental or Unproven Therapies**: Treatments, medications, or clinical trials not approved by the relevant medical authorities (e.g., FDA).
- **Section 4.3 Self-Inflicted Injuries & High-Risk Activities**: Injuries resulting from intentional self-harm or participation in extreme professional sports (e.g., skydiving, motor racing) without supplemental rider.
- **Section 4.4 Alternative Medicine**: Non-conventional treatments including acupuncture, homeopathy, naturopathy, and chiropractic adjustments exceeding 5 visits per policy year.
- **Section 4.5 Dental and Vision Routine Care**: Routine dental cleanings, extractions, orthodontic braces, eyeglasses, and laser vision correction (LASIK) are excluded under Care Plus unless an optional Dental/Vision add-on rider is active.

---

## 5. Deductible, Co-Payments, and Reimbursement Rules
- **Section 5.1 Annual Deductible**: The standard individual deductible is \$1,000 per policy year. The family deductible is \$2,500. The deductible must be satisfied before percentage reimbursement begins.
- **Section 5.2 Out-of-Pocket Maximum**: Annual maximum out-of-pocket limit is \$4,500 per individual and \$9,000 per family. Once reached, covered benefits are paid at 100% for the remainder of the policy year.
- **Section 5.3 Reimbursement Calculation**: Reimbursement = (Eligible Incurred Charges - Applicable Deductible - Non-Covered Line Items) × Co-Insurance Rate (typically 80% for in-network, 70% for out-of-network).
- **Section 5.4 Timely Filing Requirement**: Claims for reimbursement must be submitted within 90 days from the date of service or hospital discharge. Claims submitted beyond 90 days will be rejected unless extraordinary circumstances prevented submission.

---

## 6. Claim Submission Requirements
To submit a valid claim, the policyholder or provider must supply:
1. Completed and signed Claim Form (Form CL-01).
2. Itemized official hospital/clinic bill detailing all item codes and fees.
3. Attending physician’s discharge summary or diagnostic report specifying ICD-10 medical codes.
4. Original itemized pharmacy receipts with prescribed drug names and dosage for medication claims.
5. Proof of prior authorization letter if service was subject to pre-approval (e.g., advanced imaging or inpatient stay).
6. Proof of payment if seeking direct cash reimbursement for out-of-network care.

---

## 7. Claim Processing Expectations and Turnaround Times
- **Section 7.1 Initial Acknowledgment**: Submitted claims receive a unique Claim ID (e.g., CLM1001) and written acknowledgment within 2 business days of receipt.
- **Section 7.2 Standard Review Period**: Clean claims with all required documentation are processed and adjudicated within 10 to 14 business days.
- **Section 7.3 Information Request (Additional Documents)**: If documents are missing or illegible, an Information Request notice is issued within 5 business days. The claimant has 30 days to provide the requested records. The review clock resumes upon receipt of documents.
- **Section 7.4 Payment Disbursement**: Approved claims are paid via direct electronic deposit within 3 business days of approval, or paper check mailed within 7 business days.

## 8. Escalation, Appeals, and Human Support Procedures
- **Section 8.1 First-Level Claim Dispute / Appeal**: If a claim is denied or partially approved, the policyholder has 60 calendar days from the date of the Explanation of Benefits (EOB) to file an appeal.
- **Section 8.2 Escalation to Human Claims Specialist**: Policyholders are entitled to speak directly with a licensed Human Claims Specialist in the following circumstances:
  - Disputed claim denials or unexpected payment deductions.
  - Claims in "Under Review" status for more than 20 business days without status update.
  - Emergency pre-authorization requests requiring immediate verbal clearance.
  - Suspected identity theft, unauthorized claim activity, or data discrepancy.
  - Complex appeals requiring clinical peer review.
